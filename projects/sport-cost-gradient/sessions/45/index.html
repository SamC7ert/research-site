Session 45: Japan Travel Cost Validation

Summary#

Validated the German cost ranking against Japan as a second benchmark country. Researched Japanese federation licence fees and entry fees per sport using primary sources (Saitama 水連, JASF, JAAF, JCF). Identified three entry-fee buckets: 高体連-only team sports (≈0), 日連-mediated individual (¥1,000–3,000/event), and club-dominant (¥3,000–5,000+). Corrected the PPP exchange rate from market ¥160/EUR to PPP ¥134/EUR, shifting the mean JP/DE ratio from 0.77 to ~0.92. Final Spearman ρ = 0.651 with sensitivity |Δρ| ≤ 0.004.

Version: v1.09

Part 2 — PPP Exchange Rate Correction#

Sam: One thing. Since we use PPP for GDP we should use PPP rates for JPY/EUR conversion.

Claude: Significant point. The market rate (¥160/EUR) makes Japan look artificially cheap because the yen is heavily undervalued on markets relative to purchasing power. The JPY/EUR PPP rate matters for EUR columns and ratios but does not affect Spearman ρ — rank correlation is currency-neutral.

Used Chrome to access World Bank data portal. PPP conversion factors (2025): Japan 95.00 JPY per international dollar, Germany 0.71 EUR per international dollar. PPP-implied JPY/EUR = 95.00 / 0.71 = ¥134/EUR (vs market ¥160).

Effect: mean JP/DE ratio shifts from 0.77 → ~0.92 (“roughly comparable” instead of “three-quarters”). ρ unchanged.
