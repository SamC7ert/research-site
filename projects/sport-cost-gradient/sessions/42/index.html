Logical and Mathematical Inconsistencies

  • The Crossover Point Discrepancy: This is the most significant contradiction in the draft. The Abstract and Section 4.3 state that the crossover point (where the Gini effect shifts from positive to negative) falls between archery and shooting. Appendix A lists archery at €1,200 and shooting at €1,800. Section 6.4 reinforces this, explicitly calculating the crossover at €1,659. However, the caption for Figure 4 states: “Crossover at €2,654”. You need to align the figure caption with the text and the underlying math.
  • Missing Terms in Equation 3: Section 4.1 introduces the “four-way model” using Equation 3, which includes interaction terms for $\text{Gini}{i,g} \times \ln(\text{equip}s)$ and $\ln(\text{GDP}{i,g}) \times \ln(\text{facility}s)$. Immediately following the equation, the text discusses the results of the “GDP × equipment cross-term” and the “Gini × facility cross-term”. These two cross-terms are completely missing from Equation 3. You should update the equation to reflect the actual model being estimated: $$\text{points}{i,s,g} = \alpha + \beta_1 \text{Gini}{i,g} \times \ln(\text{equip}s) + \beta_2 \ln(\text{GDP}{i,g}) \times \ln(\text{facility}s) + \beta_3 \ln(\text{GDP}{i,g}) \times \ln(\text{equip}s) + \beta_4 \text{Gini}{i,g} \times \ln(\text{facility}_s) + \dots$$
  • Zero-Point Selection Argument: In Section 6.2 (Footnote 3), you mention that a Heckman selection model requires an exclusion restriction, noting that population is the natural candidate but fails because it also predicts performance. You then rely on unrestricted OLS and a Tobit model. This logic is sound, but in the Tobit results, you report $n = 5,796$ with $1,998$ observed and $3,798$ censored. Ensure that the degrees of freedom and the number of fixed effects in the Tobit model don’t suffer from the incidental parameters problem, which can bias non-linear panel models with fixed effects. Structural and Clarity Observations
  • Attributing the Residual Gap entirely to the US: In Section 7.3, you show that removing the US reduces the empirical gradient gap by 87%, and you attribute the remaining difference to the US college athletics system. However, you also note that removing Russia shifts the gradient by +0.05 and Turkey by -0.06. While the US has the largest singular impact, calling the remaining gap entirely an institutional pipeline effect of the US might be slightly overstating the precision of the simulation. Consider softening “identifying the US college athletics system as the institutional amplifier that accounts for the remaining gap” to state it accounts for the vast majority of the remaining gap.
  • Justification of Lag vs. Lead Structure: In Section 2.2, you mention testing GDP, Gini, and population lagged by one or two Olympic cycles, but opt for contemporaneous data because the $R^2$ differences were small. This is practically fine, but theoretically, the athletes competing in 2024 were shaped by the wealth distribution of 2010–2016. A reviewer might press you harder on why contemporaneous Gini is theoretically justified over childhood-era Gini, even if empirical performance is similar. Adding a brief sentence on why contemporaneous Gini proxies structural inequality well enough would tighten this up. Language and Formatting
  • Comma Splice/Run-on: In the Abstract, the sentence ending with “…excluded — the remaining gap is attributable…” uses a spaced em-dash incorrectly or creates a slight run-on. Consider splitting this into a new sentence for punchiness: “…excluded. The remaining gap is attributable to…”
  • “Wait-and-See” Phrasing: In Section 3.2, you write: “Something systematic drives inequality to help performance in some contexts and hurt it in others. The sport-cost decomposition that follows explains what that something is.”. This is a bit informal for an economics paper. Consider: “The sport-cost decomposition provides a structural explanation for this heterogeneity.”
  • AI Disclosure Versions: You mention “Claude Opus 4.6” and “Gemini 3 Pro” in the AI Disclosure. Just double-check your versioning for accuracy prior to submission (for instance, I am currently Gemini 3.1 Pro).
