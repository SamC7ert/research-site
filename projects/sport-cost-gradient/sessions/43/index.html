Session 43: Stage 2 Reproduction Round 2

Summary#

Second blind reproduction round using the corrected v1.04.1 redacted paper. Result: 94 pass, 8 fail (93.7%, adjusting for one comparison script bug).

Remaining failures were: persistence sample (reproducer error, included non-individual sports), cost classification (41 vs 47 sports, reproducer matched all common sports), Tobit convergence (statsmodels vs custom MLE), and Gini-population correlation (population vs ln(population)).

Two additional text fixes applied: specification of “the 41 sports in the regression sample” with excluded sports named, and “correlates with population” corrected to “correlates with ln(population).” A Tobit sanity check protocol was added for future reproducers.
