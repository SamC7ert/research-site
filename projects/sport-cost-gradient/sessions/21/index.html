Session 21: Year-Specific Predictors

Summary#

Replaced period-averaged GDP, population, and Gini with year-specific values from the World Bank for each Olympic year. Estonia now shows 9 unique GDP values ($5,872 in 1992 to $49,969 in 2024) instead of a single period average. Pulled data for 71 countries, patched gaps for 6 GDP-missing and 4 Gini-missing countries.

Key changes: P-values improved (more identifying variation), GDP x facility became significant at 5% (p=.036), attenuation from lag increased to 25%. Simulation comparison weakened to ~28% due to within-country GDP variation the simulation architecture cannot model.

Attempted simulation recalibration: 0 feasible solutions at 40% tolerance. Structural limitation honestly documented.

Version: v0.99
