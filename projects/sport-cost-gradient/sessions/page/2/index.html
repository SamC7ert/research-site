Session 30: Simulation Deep Dive — Mechanisms and Concentration

Summary A systematic investigation of why the simulation reproduces only 40% of the empirical gradient. The empirical gradient (β = −0.68 for individual sports, upper GDP half) is concentrated in two countries: removing the United States and Russia reduces it by 82%. The simulation shows the same concentration pattern, meaning the 40% match is driven by the same outliers rather than a broadly operating mechanism. Experiments tested whether the affordability mechanism could be strengthened: different income distribution parameters, subsistence floors, quality curves. None closed the gap substantially. The conclusion shifted from “simulation confirms the mechanism” to “simulation confirms the mechanism is insufficient — institutional channels dominate.” ...
