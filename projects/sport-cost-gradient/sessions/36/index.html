Claude: The core issue is a signal-to-noise ratio problem that’s structural, not fixable by more iterations.

How Newton works here: We want to find θ_muslim such that when the sim generates points and we run the regression on those points, the pct_muslim coefficient equals -13.05. Newton needs two things: (1) a reliable function value f(θ) = “what the sim regression gives for pct_muslim at this θ”, and (2) a reliable derivative f’(θ) = “how much does the coefficient change per unit of θ.”

The identification problem: The pct_muslim regression coefficient is estimated from variation across countries within the simulated data. In the upper GDP half, only Turkey (99% Muslim), Bahrain (70%), and Israel (14%) have meaningful Muslim population shares. The other 33 countries are essentially zero. So the regression coefficient is determined by how Turkey and Bahrain perform relative to the other 33 countries in a given seed.

The noise problem: In each seed, the Gumbel tournament produces random finishes. Turkey has maybe 15-20 individual sports × 7 game years = ~100-140 country-sport-game observations. Each of those gets tournament noise. Bahrain is smaller — maybe 30-50 observations. So the regression is fitting a coefficient to a Muslim “treatment group” of ~170 observations against a “control group” of ~1800 observations from non-Muslim countries. The noise on the treatment group’s mean is proportional to σ/√170, which is large relative to the coefficient.

Concrete numbers: At 10 seeds, the pct_muslim coefficient had SD ≈ 8-12. The target is -13.05. So the signal-to-noise ratio per evaluation is roughly 13/10 ≈ 1.3. That means a single 10-seed evaluation only distinguishes “near target” from “far from target” with about 1 standard deviation of confidence.

Why Newton diverges: Newton needs to estimate the derivative f’(θ) by computing f(θ) and f(θ+Δ). If both evaluations have SD ≈ 10, then the derivative estimate has SD ≈ √2 × 10/Δ. With Δ=1.0, the derivative SD is ~14. The actual derivative is maybe 3-6 (a reasonable quality shift changes the coefficient by a few points). So the derivative estimate has a signal-to-noise ratio below 1. Newton takes a step of -error/derivative, and when the derivative estimate is garbage, the step is garbage. Sometimes the estimated derivative is near zero or wrong sign, sending the parameter flying.

That’s what we saw: Muslim oscillated wildly (-4.0 → -2.5 → -3.1 → -3.6 → -1.7…), and Buddhist flipped from -10 during calibration to +20 at final verification.
