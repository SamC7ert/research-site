Summary#

The v1.00 pipeline was rebuilt from the v0.99 scripts with five known fixes applied: time-invariant GDP median split, country-clustered standard errors throughout, log-log population elasticity, two-lag persistence (lag2 computed as second-previous game), and religion as cultural proximity control. The pipeline produced a complete results set for comparison against v0.99.4.

Results were broadly consistent but several coefficients shifted at the second decimal. The persistence correlation dropped from r = 0.88 to r = 0.87 — the old number had been computed on a different subsample. The paper draft was updated with new values and the methodology section was tightened to specify the exact GDP split procedure.
