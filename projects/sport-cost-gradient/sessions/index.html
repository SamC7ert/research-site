Summary Built the game-level panel dataset (71 countries x 41 sports x 9 Games) with lagged Olympic points as persistence control. Scraped Barcelona 1992 and Atlanta 1996 as lag seeds. None succeeded. Then introduced rolling persistence from previous Olympic Games, which closed the gap almost completely. Affordability Curves Tested Baseline (linear): Affordability = (1 − Gini) × income share above cost threshold Moderate convex (√): Square-root transformation of affordability Strong convex (^0.3): Power-law with exponent 0.3 Lognormal standard: Full lognormal income distribution, standard σ Lognormal fat-tail (1.5σ): Fatter tails to amplify inequality effects Key Finding All five curves produced simulated gradients that were 6–19% of the empirical value.
